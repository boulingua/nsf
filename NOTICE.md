## Synthetic voice

Audio in this course is synthesised with the Piper voice `no_NO-nvcc-medium`, trained from
scratch on a **CC0** corpus, <https://www.nb.no/sprakbanken/en/resource-catalogue/oai-nb-no-sbr-15/>. CC0 carries no attribution obligation;
the credit below is given because it is deserved, not because it is owed.

Norwegian Voice Control Corpus — National Library of Norway (Nasjonalbiblioteket).
