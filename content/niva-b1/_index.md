## What B1 is for

B1 is the level at which a learner stops being a visitor to the language.
Concretely, three capacities appear:

- **Sustained text.** A paragraph that holds together — connectors, reference
  back, a beginning and an end — rather than a run of correct sentences.
- **Position-taking.** Saying what you think, saying why, and conceding a
  point without abandoning it. Norwegian discussion culture is direct and
  low-key; a learner arriving with a German or English argument register
  usually sounds either blunt or evasive, and both are teachable.
- **Register.** Norwegian's near-universal *du* misleads foreign learners into
  thinking there is no formality to manage. There is; it lives in vocabulary,
  sentence length and hedging rather than in pronouns, and B1 is where it is
  taught explicitly.

## The Nynorsk unit

Unit 12 is the course's answer to the decision recorded in the roadmap: the
course teaches Bokmål, and it owes the learner an honest account of the other
half of written Norwegian. The unit is a **recognition** unit. It sets out
what Nynorsk is and where it came from, shows the systematic correspondences
that let a Bokmål reader decode it (*ikkje* / *ikke*, *eg* / *jeg*, *kva* /
*hva*, the feminine forms, the avoidance of passive), and puts a genuine
Nynorsk text — a newspaper column, a public-service page — in front of the
learner to read.

What it does not do is ask anyone to write Nynorsk. A learner who finishes
this unit should be able to open a Nynorsk page without flinching and get its
sense. That is the realistic and useful goal, and it is the one the appendix
supports.

## The mediation unit

Unit 11 is written directly against the Companion Volume's mediation scales
and is the level's most demanding piece of authoring. Mediation is the
thinnest part of the framework for a foreign-language course at these levels:
the CV defines fewer usable B1 descriptors here than for the activity scales,
and some cells are simply empty. The course implements every B1 mediation
descriptor the CV does define, and records `no-official-descriptor` for the
rest in the coverage manifest. A silently missing scale would be a defect; a
declared empty cell is not.

## The model exam

**B1 — Modellprøve**, a sibling bundle of the final unit with the same
`unit_nr`, published as an HTML page with a committed PDF under
`static/downloads/b1/`. Four parts — *lytting*, *lesing*, *skriving*,
*muntlig* — 100 points, pass mark 60 %. The writing task is an argued text
with a stated addressee, and the oral part includes a short mediation task, so
that the two capacities the level exists for are both actually assessed.

## Where the course stops

B1 is the end of the declared `core` conformance target. **B2 and C1 are out
of scope for now** and will be reconsidered only once A1 to B1 is complete and
stable. The reason is not ambition but supply: openly-licensed and
public-domain Norwegian source material suitable for B2 work is scarce, and a
level built on thin sources would be worse than an honestly declared gap.
