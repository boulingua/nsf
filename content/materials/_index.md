Every unit ships with three downloadable artefacts: an editable **`.odp` slide
deck** for the lesson itself, a printable **PDF worksheet** for the practise
and apply steps, and **native-voice audio** with a written transcript.

Decks and worksheets are generated locally from the branded LaTeX templates of
the boulingua *slidegen* and *sheetgen* projects, in this course's signature
colour, and are committed under `static/materials/`. Continuous integration
verifies that each download exists and carries its attribution; it never builds
them, so no TeX installation sits in the deployment path. Audio lives beside
each unit and is linked from the unit page rather than from here.
