One deck per unit, built from the shared *slidegen* template in the course
accent. Each deck follows the five moves of its unit and carries speaker notes
on timing and on differentiation. The `.odp` source is editable in LibreOffice
Impress; the PDF is for projection and printing.

Decks appear here as their units are published.
