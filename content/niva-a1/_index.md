Twelve units for absolute beginners, closing with the A1 model examination.
By the end of the level a learner can introduce themselves, ask and answer
simple personal questions, handle numbers, prices and dates, describe a home,
a day and the weather, and read a short notice. The grammar spine is the
present tense, the **V2** rule and the *en/ei/et* gender system.

## Before you start

Work through the [Uttale stage](/uttale/) first. It takes one lesson and it
decides whether the next twelve units are spent learning Norwegian or
un-learning a German reading of Norwegian.

## The recurring cast

One small ensemble carries the whole course, so that vocabulary spirals
instead of resetting every unit:

- **Maja**, 16, from Oslo. Lives on the east side of the city, takes the
  T-bane to school, and is the unit-one narrator.
- **Jonas**, her older brother, 19, studying in **Bergen** — which gives the
  course its second city, its rain, and its dialect contrast.
- **Lea**, an exchange pupil from Germany in Maja's class. She is the
  learner's proxy: she asks the questions a beginner actually has, and she
  gets them answered in Norwegian.
- **The family hytte**, a cabin two hours out of Oslo. It appears first in
  Unit 4 and returns at every level — the single most Norwegian setting the
  course has.

## The twelve units

| # | Unit | Focus | Skill emphasis |
|--:|:-----|:------|:---------------|
|  1 | Hei! — hilsener og presentasjon | Greetings, name, where you are from | Speaking (interaction) |
|  2 | Tall, klokka og datoer | Numbers, time, dates, prices | Listening |
|  3 | Familien min | Family words, possessives, *min/mitt/mine* | Reading |
|  4 | Hjemme og på hytta | Rooms, furniture, the cabin | Writing |
|  5 | Mat og handling | Food, shopping, ordering, quantities | Speaking (interaction) |
|  6 | En vanlig dag | Daily routine, time adverbs, frequency | Listening |
|  7 | Vær og årstider | Weather, the four seasons, clothing | Listening |
|  8 | I byen — veien dit | Town, directions, public transport | Reading |
|  9 | Fritid | Free time, sport, *friluftsliv* in outline | Speaking (production) |
| 10 | Presens og ordstilling | Present tense and the **V2** rule | Language awareness |
| 11 | En, ei, et | Noun gender, indefinite and definite forms | Language awareness |
| 12 | Nabospråk — dansk og svensk | Recognising the neighbouring languages | Reading · Intercultural |

Units 10 and 11 are deliberately grammar-led rather than theme-led. Everything
before them uses the present tense and the gender system without naming them;
these two units name what the learner has already been doing, which is the
point at which it becomes usable outside the sentences it was met in.

Unit 12 is the first appearance of the **Scandinavian thread**. It is a
reading and recognition unit — a Danish sign, a Swedish timetable, the same
sentence in three spellings — and it is explicit that the skill being built is
receptive. Nothing at A1 asks a learner to produce Danish or Swedish.

## The model exam

The level closes with **A1 — Modellprøve**, a first-class sibling bundle of
Unit 12 sharing its `unit_nr`, published as an HTML page with a committed PDF
under `static/downloads/a1/`. Four parts — *lytting*, *lesing*, *skriving*,
*muntlig* — 100 points in total, pass mark 60 %. The oral part is a paired
task, because at A1 a partner is scaffolding, not competition.

## Curriculum alignment

Every unit carries `curriculum.framework: cefr`, `cefr_level: A1` and a list
of `cefr_can_do` statements that reference descriptor IDs of the form
`A1.{DOMAIN}.{SCALE}.{SEQ}` from the shared boulingua curriculum registry.
The A1 anchor scales are the reception scales (oral and reading
comprehension), oral and written production, the three interaction scales
(conversation, information exchange, written interaction), general linguistic
range, and sociolinguistic appropriateness. Mediation and plurilingual scales
are implemented wherever the Companion Volume defines an A1 descriptor and
recorded as `no-official-descriptor` where it does not.
